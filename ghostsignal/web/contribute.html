Share your Amazon orders

Help build a product database by sharing what you've bought. It takes about five minutes.

1. Install the browser extension Easiest

Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc on a computer.

  1. Download the extension
  2. Unzip the file you downloaded.
  3. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Go to your Amazon orders, click the GhostSignal icon in the toolbar (the puzzle piece if it's hidden), then Send my orders.

2. Or paste a script (no install)

For anyone comfortable with the browser console.

  2. Open your Amazon orders and press Cmd+Opt+J (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+J (Windows).
  3. Paste and press Enter. Chrome may ask you to type allow pasting first. Leave the tab open until it says it's done.

3. Or upload a file

The file from the script above, or the order history CSV from Amazon's "Request Your Data".

What's shared: product name, ASIN, order date, quantity and price. Addresses, payment details and gift messages are never read or stored. Your orders are filed under the nickname the person who invited you chose, not your name or email. Ask them if you want your data removed.
